| K | Scheduler retrievability model fix | Avg Familiarity sits around 60% despite 25 reviews/day, and a character displayed below 20% familiarity is recalled correctly ~80% of the time — the exponential retrievability curve (`R = exp(-elapsed/stability)`, duplicated in `scheduler.ts` and `words.shared.utils.tsx`) decays far faster than real recall, and its interval target schedules reviews ~13× sooner than the corrected math implies is needed, producing a due backlog (~600 of ~800 words) far beyond 25/day throughput.
