# Zongzi kitchen scene

Reference images (in attached order):

1. Empty kitchen. The base room. Keep its layout, camera angle and proportions exactly.
2. Shrewy. The chef character, a chubby grey tabby cat in a white chef hat and a sage-green apron. Keep the look on model.
3. Zongzi. The finished dish. A pyramid-shaped zongzi wrapped in green bamboo leaves and tied with string in a bow, with a smiling face.
4. Sticky Rice. A woven basket of white sticky rice with a cloth tag on the front.
5. Bamboo Leaves. A bundle of long green bamboo leaves tied at the stems.
6. Sugar. A glass jar of white sugar with a checked cloth lid tied with a bow.
7. Zongzi String. A coil of cream cotton string tied with an orange bow.
8. Crystal Pear Dew scene. The finished example of this kind of image. Follow how it adds food props, sparkles, steam and Shrewy cooking at the island to the empty room. This new image is the same idea for a different recipe.

Prompt:

Paint the kitchen from image 1 in the same soft, hand-painted isometric storybook style as image 8. Use the same warm cream and butter-yellow palette, sage-green accents, gentle brown linework and twinkling sparkles. Today the kitchen is making Zongzi (粽子).

Room: keep everything from image 1 in the same place and at the same size. That means the smiling green fridge, the ladder, the cabinets, the smiling yellow stove and oven, the recipe book on its stand, the window and sink, the rug, the island with its green towel, and the tall shelf on the right. Fill the right-hand shelf the same way image 8 does: bubble tea, a cake slice, flan and a ramen bowl.

Shrewy (image 2): standing on a small wooden stool at the island, back half-turned to us, in the same spot and pose as in image 8. Keep Shrewy at the island, never in front of the stove or oven, and don't let them hide any appliance or ingredient. They fold a green bamboo leaf into a cone, ready to fill it with sticky rice. Two finished zongzi sit on a small wooden board beside them.

The stove is the star. A big pot on the front burner bubbles away with wrapped zongzi bobbing in it. Soft steam and golden sparkles rise from it. Keep the stove's smiling face. Make the pot big enough to see clearly and keep it unblocked, because in the game the ingredients are dragged into it.

Oven: closed, just its smiling face.

Ingredients: place the four ingredients from images 4–7 so each one stands clearly apart from the others and is easy to pick out:

- Sticky rice basket (4) on the island near the front-left corner.
- Bamboo leaves (5) on the island's front corner, near Shrewy.
- Sugar jar (6) on the back counter, left of the sink.
- Coil of string (7) on the back counter, right of the sink.

Draw each one to match its reference, small and in scale with the room. Keep every ingredient off the fridge, recipe book, stove, oven and right-hand shelf.

Finished zongzi: put one zongzi from image 3 on a small plate on the counter beside the recipe book, with a few sparkles around it.

Mood: festive and fresh, like the Dragon Boat Festival (端午节). It's still the same bright daytime room. An optional subtle touch: a small bundle of green mugwort hanging by the window.

Technical:

- Same framing and aspect ratio as image 8: 1600 × 1391 px, about 8:7.
- Plain cream background outside the room cutaway.
- No text, labels or watermark anywhere, including on jars, bottles and packets (leave their labels blank).
- Don't move or resize the fridge, recipe book, stove, oven or right-hand shelf, because the game puts tap targets over them.
