# Donut kitchen scene

Reference images (in attached order):

1. Empty kitchen. The base room. Keep its layout, camera angle and proportions exactly.
2. Shrewy. The chef character, a chubby grey tabby cat in a white chef hat and a sage-green apron. Keep the look on model.
3. Donut. The finished dish. A golden ring donut with thick white glaze dripping down its sides, a few sparkles and a smiling face.
4. Flour. A cream burlap sack of white flour, open at the top, with a golden wheat emblem.
5. Milk. A glass milk jar with a pale blue lid and a little cow-face label.
6. Egg. An open cardboard carton full of brown eggs.
7. Sugar. A glass jar of white sugar with a checked cloth lid tied with a bow.
8. Crystal Pear Dew scene. The finished example of this kind of image. Follow how it adds food props, sparkles, steam and Shrewy cooking at the island to the empty room. This new image is the same idea for a different recipe.

Prompt:

Paint the kitchen from image 1 in the same soft, hand-painted isometric storybook style as image 8. Use the same warm cream and butter-yellow palette, sage-green accents, gentle brown linework and twinkling sparkles. Today the kitchen is making Donut (甜甜圈).

Room: keep everything from image 1 in the same place and at the same size. That means the smiling green fridge, the ladder, the cabinets, the smiling yellow stove and oven, the recipe book on its stand, the window and sink, the rug, the island with its green towel, and the tall shelf on the right. Fill the right-hand shelf the same way image 8 does: bubble tea, a cake slice, flan and a ramen bowl.

Shrewy (image 2): standing on a small wooden stool at the island, back half-turned to us, in the same spot and pose as in image 8. Keep Shrewy at the island, never in front of the stove or oven, and don't let them hide any appliance or ingredient. They stir soft dough in a large celadon mixing bowl with a wooden spoon. A round donut cutter and two cut dough rings sit on a floured board beside the bowl.

The oven is the star. Its window glows warm gold, with a tray of golden ring donuts from image 3 baking inside. Golden sparkles and a soft wisp of warm steam rise from the oven door. Keep the oven's smiling face.

Make the mixing bowl big and clearly visible in front of Shrewy, not hidden by their paws, because in the game the ingredients are dragged into it.

Stovetop: no soup pot. Leave it clear or put a small, simple kettle on it.

Ingredients: place the four ingredients from images 4–7 so each one stands clearly apart from the others and is easy to pick out:

- Flour sack (4) on the island near the front-left corner.
- Egg carton (6) on the island's front corner, near Shrewy.
- Milk jar (5) on the back counter, left of the sink.
- Sugar jar (7) on the back counter, right of the sink.

Draw each one to match its reference, small and in scale with the room. Keep every ingredient off the fridge, recipe book, stove, oven and right-hand shelf.

Finished donut: put one glazed donut from image 3 on a small plate on the counter beside the recipe book, with a few sparkles around it.

Mood: playful and sweet, like a Saturday treat. It's still the same bright daytime room.

Technical:

- Same framing and aspect ratio as image 8: 1600 × 1391 px, about 8:7.
- Plain cream background outside the room cutaway.
- No text, labels or watermark anywhere, including on jars, bottles and packets (leave their labels blank).
- Don't move or resize the fridge, recipe book, stove, oven or right-hand shelf, because the game puts tap targets over them.
