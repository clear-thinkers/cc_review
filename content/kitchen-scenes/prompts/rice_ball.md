# Rice Ball kitchen scene

Reference images (in attached order):

1. Empty kitchen. The base room. Keep its layout, camera angle and proportions exactly.
2. Shrewy. The chef character, a chubby grey tabby cat in a white chef hat and a sage-green apron. Keep the look on model.
3. Rice Ball. The finished dish. A triangle rice ball wrapped with a strip of nori, with a smiling face.
4. Steam Rice. A wooden bowl heaped with fluffy white rice.
5. Seaweed. A stack of dark-green nori sheets on a folded cream cloth.
6. Salt. A cork-topped glass jar of white salt tied with a twine bow.
7. Crystal Pear Dew scene. The finished example of this kind of image. Follow how it adds food props, sparkles, steam and Shrewy cooking at the island to the empty room. This new image is the same idea for a different recipe.

Prompt:

Paint the kitchen from image 1 in the same soft, hand-painted isometric storybook style as image 7. Use the same warm cream and butter-yellow palette, sage-green accents, gentle brown linework and twinkling sparkles. Today the kitchen is making Rice Ball (饭团).

Room: keep everything from image 1 in the same place and at the same size. That means the smiling green fridge, the ladder, the cabinets, the smiling yellow stove and oven, the recipe book on its stand, the window and sink, the rug, the island with its green towel, and the tall shelf on the right. Fill the right-hand shelf the same way image 7 does: bubble tea, a cake slice, flan and a ramen bowl.

Shrewy (image 2): standing on a small wooden stool at the island, back half-turned to us, in the same spot and pose as in image 7. Keep Shrewy at the island, never in front of the stove or oven, and don't let them hide any appliance or ingredient. They shape a triangle of rice between their paws over a large, shallow wooden tub of fluffy white rice. Two finished rice balls sit on a small wooden board beside the tub.

The stove is the star. A pot of rice steams gently on the front burner, its lid tilted, with soft steam and golden sparkles rising. Keep the stove's smiling face.

Make the mixing bowl big and clearly visible in front of Shrewy, not hidden by their paws, because in the game the ingredients are dragged into it.

Oven: closed, just its smiling face.

Ingredients: place the three ingredients from images 4–6 so each one stands clearly apart from the others and is easy to pick out:

- Rice bowl (4) on the island near the front-left corner.
- Salt jar (6) on the island's front corner, near Shrewy.
- Nori sheets (5) on the back counter, left of the sink.

Draw each one to match its reference, small and in scale with the room. Keep every ingredient off the fridge, recipe book, stove, oven and right-hand shelf.

Finished rice ball: put one rice ball from image 3 on a small plate on the counter beside the recipe book, with a few sparkles around it.

Mood: cheerful, like packing a picnic lunch. It's still the same bright daytime room.

Technical:

- Same framing and aspect ratio as image 7: 1600 × 1391 px, about 8:7.
- Plain cream background outside the room cutaway.
- No text, labels or watermark anywhere, including on jars, bottles and packets (leave their labels blank).
- Don't move or resize the fridge, recipe book, stove, oven or right-hand shelf, because the game puts tap targets over them.
