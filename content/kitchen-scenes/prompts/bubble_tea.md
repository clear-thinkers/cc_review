# Bubble Tea kitchen scene

Reference images (in attached order):

1. Empty kitchen. The base room. Keep its layout, camera angle and proportions exactly.
2. Shrewy. The chef character, a chubby grey tabby cat in a white chef hat and a sage-green apron. Keep the look on model.
3. Bubble Tea. The finished dish. A clear cup of caramel milk tea with dark tapioca pearls at the bottom, a green dome lid, a green straw and a little smiling face.
4. Milk. A glass milk jar with a pale blue lid and a little cow-face label.
5. Tapioca Pearls. A burlap sack tied with twine, overflowing with glossy dark-brown tapioca pearls.
6. Crystal Pear Dew scene. The finished example of this kind of image. Follow how it adds food props, sparkles, steam and Shrewy cooking at the island to the empty room. This new image is the same idea for a different recipe.

Prompt:

Paint the kitchen from image 1 in the same soft, hand-painted isometric storybook style as image 6. Use the same warm cream and butter-yellow palette, sage-green accents, gentle brown linework and twinkling sparkles. Today the kitchen is making Bubble Tea (珍珠奶茶).

Room: keep everything from image 1 in the same place and at the same size. That means the smiling green fridge, the ladder, the cabinets, the smiling yellow stove and oven, the recipe book on its stand, the window and sink, the rug, the island with its green towel, and the tall shelf on the right. Fill the right-hand shelf the same way image 6 does: bubble tea, a cake slice, flan and a ramen bowl.

Shrewy (image 2): standing on a small wooden stool at the island, back half-turned to us, in the same spot and pose as in image 6. Keep Shrewy at the island, never in front of the stove or oven, and don't let them hide any appliance or ingredient. They hold a tall clear cup in one paw and a long spoon in the other, ready to pour. A green dome lid and a straw wait beside them on the island.

The stove is the star. A small saucepan on the front burner bubbles with caramel-coloured milk tea, dark tapioca pearls swirling in it. Golden sparkles and a soft curl of sweet steam rise from it. Keep the stove's smiling face. Make the pot big enough to see clearly and keep it unblocked, because in the game the ingredients are dragged into it.

Oven: closed, just its smiling face.

Ingredients: place both ingredients from images 4–5 so each one stands clearly apart from the other and is easy to pick out:

- Milk jar (4) on the back counter, left of the sink.
- Tapioca pearl sack (5) on the island near the front-left corner.

Draw each one to match its reference, small and in scale with the room. Keep every ingredient off the fridge, recipe book, stove, oven and right-hand shelf.

Finished bubble tea: put one bubble tea from image 3 on the counter beside the recipe book, with a few sparkles around it.

Mood: cheerful and cosy, like an afternoon tea break. It's still the same bright daytime room.

Technical:

- Same framing and aspect ratio as image 6: 1600 × 1391 px, about 8:7.
- Plain cream background outside the room cutaway.
- No text, labels or watermark anywhere, including on jars, bottles and packets (leave their labels blank).
- Don't move or resize the fridge, recipe book, stove, oven or right-hand shelf, because the game puts tap targets over them.
