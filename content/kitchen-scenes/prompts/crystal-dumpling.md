Prompt:

Room: keep everything from image 1 in the same place and at the same size. That means the smiling green fridge, the ladder, the cabinets, the smiling yellow stove and oven, the recipe book on its stand, the window and sink, the rug, the island with its green towel, and the tall shelf on the right. Fill the right-hand shelf the same way image 8 does: bubble tea, a cake slice, flan and a ramen bowl.

Shrewy (image 2): standing on a small wooden stool at the island, back half-turned to us, in the same spot and pose as in image 8. Keep Shrewy at the island, never in front of the stove or oven, and don't let them hide any appliance or ingredient. They pleat a translucent dumpling wrapper over a large celadon mixing bowl of pink prawn filling. Two or three finished dumplings sit on a small bamboo mat beside the bowl.

The stove is the star. A dark woven bamboo steamer from image 3 sits on a pot on the front burner, with soft clouds of steam and golden sparkles rising from under its lid. Keep the stove's smiling face.

Make the mixing bowl big and clearly visible in front of Shrewy, not hidden by their paws, because in the game the ingredients are dragged into it.

Oven: closed, just its smiling face.

Ingredients: place the four ingredients from images 4–7 so each one stands clearly apart from the others and is easy to pick out:

- Starch jar (4) on the island near the front-left corner.
- Prawn basket (5) on the island's front corner, near Shrewy.
- Bamboo shoot crate (6) on the back counter, left of the sink.
- Sesame oil cruet (7) on the back counter, right of the sink.

Draw each one to match its reference, small and in scale with the room. Keep every ingredient off the fridge, recipe book, stove, oven and right-hand shelf.

Finished dumplings: put one small steamer of crystal dumplings from image 3 on the counter beside the recipe book, with a few sparkles around it.

Mood: elegant and a little magical, like a treasured dim sum morning. It's still the same bright daytime room, and the shimmer comes from the food.
