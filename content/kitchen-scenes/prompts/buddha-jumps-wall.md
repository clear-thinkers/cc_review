# Buddha Jumps Wall kitchen scene

Reference images (in attached order):

1. Empty kitchen. The base room. Keep its layout, camera angle and proportions exactly.
2. Shrewy. The chef character, a chubby grey tabby cat in a white chef hat and a sage-green apron. Keep the look on model.
3. Buddha Jumps Wall. The finished dish. A blue-and-white porcelain jar tied with red cord, its lid tilted open over rich golden broth with abalone and mushrooms, on a round bamboo mat.
4. Golden Rim Abalone. A glossy golden abalone in a celadon flower-shaped dish on a little bamboo stand, with two small pearls.
5. Sun-Dried Scallop. Two plump golden dried scallops in a shallow bamboo basket.
6. Mountain Snow Morel. A frosted morel mushroom on pine needles on a round slice of wood.
7. Aged Shaoxing Dew. A round terracotta wine jar with a jade-green knob on its lid, on a small wooden stand, glowing softly.
8. Crystal Pear Dew scene. The finished example of this kind of image. Follow how it adds food props, sparkles, steam and Shrewy cooking at the island to the empty room. This new image is the same idea for a different recipe.

Prompt:

Paint the kitchen from image 1 in the same soft, hand-painted isometric storybook style as image 8. Use the same warm cream and butter-yellow palette, sage-green accents, gentle brown linework and twinkling sparkles. Today the kitchen is making Buddha Jumps Wall (佛跳墙).

Room: keep everything from image 1 in the same place and at the same size. That means the smiling green fridge, the ladder, the cabinets, the smiling yellow stove and oven, the recipe book on its stand, the window and sink, the rug, the island with its green towel, and the tall shelf on the right. Fill the right-hand shelf the same way image 8 does: bubble tea, a cake slice, flan and a ramen bowl.

Shrewy (image 2): standing on a small wooden stool at the island, back half-turned to us, in the same spot and pose as in image 8. Keep Shrewy at the island, never in front of the stove or oven, and don't let them hide any appliance or ingredient. They hold a small ladle and turn to sniff the rich golden steam drifting over from the stove, eyes closed happily.

The stove is the star. A big blue-and-white porcelain jar like the one in image 3 simmers on the front burner, its lid tilted open, with rich golden steam and golden sparkles rising from it. Keep the stove's smiling face. Make the pot big enough to see clearly and keep it unblocked, because in the game the ingredients are dragged into it.

Oven: closed, just its smiling face.

Ingredients: place the four ingredients from images 4–7 so each one stands clearly apart from the others and is easy to pick out:

- Abalone dish (4) on the island near the front-left corner.
- Scallop basket (5) on the island's front corner, near Shrewy.
- Morel on its wood slice (6) on the back counter, left of the sink.
- Shaoxing wine jar (7) on the back counter, right of the sink.

Draw each one to match its reference, small and in scale with the room. Keep every ingredient off the fridge, recipe book, stove, oven and right-hand shelf.

Finished dish: put one small porcelain jar of Buddha Jumps Wall from image 3 on its bamboo mat on the counter beside the recipe book, with a few sparkles around it.

Mood: grand and festive, like a Chinese New Year feast. It's still the same bright daytime room, and the golden glow comes from the food. An optional subtle touch: one small red paper cutting (窗花) on the window.

Technical:

- Same framing and aspect ratio as image 8: 1600 × 1391 px, about 8:7.
- Plain cream background outside the room cutaway.
- No text, labels or watermark anywhere, including on jars, bottles and packets (leave their labels blank).
- Don't move or resize the fridge, recipe book, stove, oven or right-hand shelf, because the game puts tap targets over them.
