# Plain Noodles kitchen scene

Reference images (in attached order):

1. Empty kitchen. The base room. Keep its layout, camera angle and proportions exactly.
2. Shrewy. The chef character, a chubby grey tabby cat in a white chef hat and a sage-green apron. Keep the look on model.
3. Plain Noodles. The finished dish. A white bowl with a blue patterned rim, holding thin noodles in clear broth with chopped scallions, chopsticks lifting some noodles, and a smiling face. Ignore the Chinese title above it.
4. Noodles. A neat nest of fresh noodles on a folded cream cloth.
5. Broth. A cork-stoppered glass bottle of golden broth with a herb sprig.
6. Crystal Pear Dew scene. The finished example of this kind of image. Follow how it adds food props, sparkles, steam and Shrewy cooking at the island to the empty room. This new image is the same idea for a different recipe.

Prompt:

Paint the kitchen from image 1 in the same soft, hand-painted isometric storybook style as image 6. Use the same warm cream and butter-yellow palette, sage-green accents, gentle brown linework and twinkling sparkles. Today the kitchen is making Plain Noodles (阳春面).

Room: keep everything from image 1 in the same place and at the same size. That means the smiling green fridge, the ladder, the cabinets, the smiling yellow stove and oven, the recipe book on its stand, the window and sink, the rug, the island with its green towel, and the tall shelf on the right. Fill the right-hand shelf the same way image 6 does: bubble tea, a cake slice, flan and a ramen bowl.

Shrewy (image 2): standing on a small wooden stool at the island, back half-turned to us, in the same spot and pose as in image 6. Keep Shrewy at the island, never in front of the stove or oven, and don't let them hide any appliance or ingredient. They sprinkle chopped scallions into an empty blue-rimmed bowl, with a pair of chopsticks resting beside it.

The stove is the star. A pot on the front burner simmers with clear golden broth, thin noodles swirling in it. Soft steam and golden sparkles rise from it. Keep the stove's smiling face. Make the pot big enough to see clearly and keep it unblocked, because in the game the ingredients are dragged into it.

Oven: closed, just its smiling face.

Ingredients: place both ingredients from images 4–5 so each one stands clearly apart from the other and is easy to pick out:

- Noodle nest (4) on the island near the front-left corner.
- Broth bottle (5) on the back counter, left of the sink.

Draw each one to match its reference, small and in scale with the room. Keep every ingredient off the fridge, recipe book, stove, oven and right-hand shelf.

Finished noodles: put one bowl of noodles from image 3 on the counter beside the recipe book, with a few sparkles around it.

Mood: simple and comforting, like a quiet home-cooked lunch. It's still the same bright daytime room.
